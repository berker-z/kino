Faking the
Trinitron

You were right: it's synthetic. Three things in the 4K master give it away. The stripes are in phase across R, G and B (a real aperture grille puts the three colors side by side). The grade is a single luminance-to-color curve with almost no scatter. And the focus falls off only toward the left and right edges; the top stays sharp.

So the treatment is a shader, Kino.screenPass. Every image below has the reference in the top left and three tries (A, B, C) in the other quadrants. The inputs are grayscale plates I made from their frames (stripes blurred out, grade inverted), so the comparison is like for like. The plates are a bit softer than their originals, so ignore small differences in letter sharpness.

Numbers are local stripe contrast (std of a high-pass) in a few regions, theirs vs ours. They're what I tuned against; your eye is the actual judge.

Frames come from compositions/cyanotype-lab, rendered at 3840×2160 to match the reference. Images are local only (research/*/media/ is gitignored).
